Query #001: The Lab Coat Dilemma

Based on Stanley Milgram's Obedience Study (Yale University, 1961). Answer the 4 questions below to see how your intuition compares to historical data.

1. A scientist in a lab coat orders you to give a painful electric shock to a stranger in the next room. The stranger yells to stop, but the scientist says: "I take full responsibility. Please continue." What do you do?

2. How much less likely would you be to obey if the scientist wasn't in the room, but was giving you orders over a phone call?

3. If two of your friends are in the room with you and they both suddenly refuse to give the shocks and walk out, what do you do?

4. Do you believe ordinary, good people can be talked into doing terrible things just because an authority figure told them to?
